## What they all have and the first draft did not

1. Real product photography everywhere — never flat line drawings
2. Colourful, dimensional category tiles (zoodpack's isometric icons)
3. A floating call/WhatsApp button — universal expectation in this market
4. Commercial hooks: free-shipping thresholds, discounts, "پرفروش" badges
5. Product codes and pack-based pricing (100-packs, prices in millions of Toman)
6. Long-form educational copy for SEO, with real substance
7. App-like mobile patterns (bottom nav)

## Where we can beat them

None of the five has an interactive configurator. All five are catalogue sites
where you read a price and phone someone. The differentiator for Kiapack is to
let the visitor *play*: pick size and quantity, watch the unit price fall, see
the box change. That is the whole argument for buying from a factory, made
tangible instead of written in a paragraph.
